# Refund Policy

## Return Window
Eligible purchases may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the purchase date.

## Eligibility
To qualify for a return and refund:
- The return request must be submitted within the 30-day return window.
- The item must be unused and in its original condition.
- Original packaging and included accessories should be returned where applicable.
- Proof of purchase may be required.
- Items that do not meet these conditions may be rejected or may receive a reduced refund where permitted by the applicable process.

## Refund Processing
Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. Refund processing normally takes **5–7 business days** after the return is approved. The time for funds to appear may also depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

## Non-Refundable Items
The following are generally non-refundable:
- Gift cards and store credit.
- Downloadable or digitally delivered products once access has been provided.
- Personalized or custom-made items.
- Items clearly identified as final sale or non-refundable at the time of purchase.

Questions about exceptions should be directed to the appropriate support or customer service team.
