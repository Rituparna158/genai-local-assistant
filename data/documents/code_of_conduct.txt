CODE OF CONDUCT

Purpose
The Code of Conduct establishes expectations for professional, respectful, and ethical behavior across the workplace.

PROFESSIONAL WORKPLACE ETHICS
Employees are expected to act honestly, professionally, respectfully, and responsibly. Company resources, confidential information, and business relationships must be handled appropriately. Employees should communicate respectfully and make decisions in good faith.

ANTI-HARASSMENT
The company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, or other inappropriate conduct in the workplace. This expectation applies to interactions with coworkers, managers, customers, contractors, and other people representing or interacting with the company.

REPORTING MISCONDUCT
Employees who experience or observe suspected misconduct should report it through an appropriate reporting channel, such as their manager, Human Resources, compliance contact, or another designated reporting mechanism. Reports should be made promptly and in good faith. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest that could affect—or reasonably appear to affect—their professional judgment. Disclosures should be made to the appropriate manager, Human Resources, compliance contact, or other designated company representative so the situation can be reviewed and managed appropriately.

COMPLIANCE
Employees are expected to cooperate with workplace investigations and comply with applicable company policies and procedures.
