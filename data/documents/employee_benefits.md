# Employee Benefits

The company provides a benefits program intended to support employee health, professional development, wellbeing, and long-term financial planning.

## Health Insurance
Eligible employees have access to comprehensive health insurance coverage under the company's benefits plan. Coverage, eligibility, dependents, exclusions, and enrollment requirements are governed by the current plan documents.

## Learning & Development
Eligible employees receive an annual **$1,000 learning and development budget** for approved courses, certifications, conferences, books, and other qualifying professional-development expenses. Reimbursement is subject to the company's approval and expense procedures.

## Gym Membership
Eligible employees may receive reimbursement for qualifying gym membership expenses, subject to the company's reimbursement rules and any applicable limits or documentation requirements.

## 401(k) Matching
The company provides a **401(k) matching contribution of up to 4%** of eligible compensation, subject to plan eligibility, employee contributions, vesting requirements, and the official retirement plan documents.
