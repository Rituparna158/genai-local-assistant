IT SECURITY POLICY

Purpose
This policy establishes minimum security practices for protecting company systems, accounts, devices, and information.

PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
- Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
- Employees must not share passwords with other people.
- Passwords should be unique to company accounts and should not be reused across unrelated services.
- Suspected credential compromise must be reported promptly to the IT/security team.

MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for company accounts and supported systems. Employees must use the approved authentication method and must not approve authentication requests they did not initiate.

PHISHING REPORTING
Employees should treat unexpected links, attachments, login requests, payment requests, and credential requests as potentially suspicious.
- Do not enter credentials or open attachments when a message appears suspicious.
- Report suspected phishing through the company's approved security reporting mechanism or to the IT/security team.
- If credentials were entered into a suspicious site, report the incident immediately and follow the security team's instructions.

CLEAN DESK
Employees must keep sensitive documents and information secure when away from their workspace. Confidential papers should not be left unattended on desks, printers, or meeting-room surfaces. Devices should be locked when unattended.

UNAUTHORIZED USB DRIVES
Employees must not connect unauthorized USB drives or other removable storage devices to company computers or systems. Only company-approved or explicitly authorized removable media may be used.

COMPLIANCE
Employees are expected to follow these requirements and cooperate with security investigations and remediation activities.
